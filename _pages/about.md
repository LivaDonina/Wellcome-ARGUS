ARGUS, named after the hundred-eyed giant of Greek myth, reflects the project's goal of building an imaging technology that can continuously "see" throughout the living body. Funded by the Wellcome Trust's Bioimaging Technology Development Award, this Imperial–KCL–TU Delft collaboration is developing next-generation ultrasound combined with genetically encoded acoustic reporters, a "GFP for ultrasound", to visualise cellular function non-invasively in 3D at sub-diffraction resolution. The platform will be validated in metabolic research, resolving key questions about pancreatic beta cell mass and gut hormone signalling. The project will also produce mouse lines, protocols and training to make the technology widely accessible.
